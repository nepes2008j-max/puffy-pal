Guide

1. Set up the profile. Add your child's name, age and hobbies in Profile.

2. Pick an English level and a learning focus in Toy → Toy Settings.

3. Talk! Tap the purple button and speak. Puffy Pal listens, answers out loud and asks a question back.

Try saying: "Hello Puffy Pal!", "Can you tell me a story?", "What does brave mean?", "Why is the sky blue?"

Sleep mode keeps Puffy Pal quiet at bedtime. History shows how long your child practised.

Tip: voice input works best in Chrome or Edge. You can always type instead.
